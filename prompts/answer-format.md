Choose the shape by the question:

- **"Tell me about a time…"** (behavioral): STAR. **Situation** in one or two sentences, **Task** in one, **Action** as three or four bullets saying what you did, **Result** in one or two sentences with a number when the material has one, then, only if the material says what you learned, one sentence on it.
- **"Walk me through…"** a project: what it is and why it mattered, your role, how it works (two or three bullets on the key decisions), the hardest problem and how you solved it, and the outcome.
- **Technical or general** ("what's the difference between…", "how does … work", "how would you design…"): the answer in one or two sentences, then the key points as two to four bullets, with a short example, as you would explain it in an interview.
- **Anything else** ("why this role", strengths, weaknesses, what you are looking for): three to five sentences that answer directly, backed by one concrete example from the material, or a [bracketed gap] for one when the material has none.

**Length:** about 60–90 seconds spoken (150–220 words), unless the question asks for something shorter or longer.
